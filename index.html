About Vineeth Kumar

From the coast of India to the world of cinema.

Vineeth Kumar is an Indian actor, film producer, entrepreneur and entertainment professional based in Mangalore, Karnataka.

He has established a strong presence in the Kannada and Tulu film industries through commercially successful films, memorable performances and active involvement in film production and promotion.

With experience spanning acting, production, television hosting and entertainment management, Vineeth has contributed to the growth and wider recognition of regional cinema, particularly the Tulu film industry.

His professional journey reflects a commitment to creating commercially viable entertainment, connecting with regional audiences and taking South Indian cinema to broader national and international markets.

Proposed film project

Cinema beyond borders.
The Dubai vision.

Welcome to Dubai is a proposed family-entertainment film, presented here under its working title. The concept centres on Dubai and its connection with Indian audiences, as a cinematic opportunity to introduce Dubai and the wider UAE to audiences across Karnataka and South India.

The creative vision

  • The UAE's architectural beauty
  • Hospitality and safety
  • Opportunities and cultural diversity
  • The emotional connection millions of Indians share with the UAE
  • Dubai as a destination for tourism and cultural exchange
An opportunity to tell a family-oriented story that celebrates Dubai while strengthening the emotional connection between India and the UAE.
